# FitMe User Guide

Welcome to FitMe, your personal workout and nutrition tracking website! This guide will help you get started and make the most of the platform.

## 1. Account Management

### Registration
1.  Navigate to the 'Register' page.
2.  Fill in your desired username, password, email, and optional details like sex, age, weight (kg), and height (cm).
3.  Click 'Register'. You'll be automatically logged in.

### Login / Logout
*   **Login:** Go to the 'Login' page and enter your username and password.
*   **Logout:** Click the 'Logout' link in the navigation bar.

### Updating Your Profile
1.  Once logged in, click 'Profile' in the navigation bar.
2.  Here you can see your current details. Click 'Edit Profile'.
3.  Update your personal information:
    *   **Basic Info:** Username, email, first/last name.
    *   **Physical Stats:** Sex, Age, Weight (kg), Height (cm). **Keep these updated for accurate calculator results!**
    *   **Activity Level:** Select how active you typically are. This is used for calorie estimations.
    *   **Diseases:** Select any pre-existing conditions. This helps filter out exercises that might not be suitable for you.
4.  Click 'Save Changes'.

### Your Dashboard
*   Access the 'Dashboard' from the navigation bar.
*   **Profile Summary:** A quick view of your key profile details.
*   **Latest Health Metrics:** Displays your latest calculated BMI, BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate), and estimated Daily Calorie Needs. These are automatically updated when you save changes to relevant fields in your profile (weight, height, age, sex, activity level).
*   **Weight Progress:** A graph showing your weight over time, based on the weight you enter in your profile or via 'Manually Add/Update Today's Health Data' on your profile page. You need at least two entries to see the graph.

## 2. Workouts

### Browsing and Filtering Exercises
*   **Categories:** Click 'Workouts' in the navigation. You'll see a list of categories (e.g., Arms, Chest, Warm-up, Cool-down, Kegel Exercises). Click a category to see exercises within it.
*   **All Exercises:** From the categories page, click 'View All Exercises' or navigate directly via a main 'Exercises' link if available.
*   **Interactive Muscle Map:** Find this link (often within the Workouts section). Click on a muscle group name to see exercises primarily targeting that muscle.
*   **Filters:** On the 'All Exercises' page, use the filter form to narrow down exercises by:
    *   Name
    *   Equipment needed (select 'None' for bodyweight)
    *   Primary muscle group
    *   Difficulty level
    *   Body focus (Upper, Lower, Core, etc.)
    *   Whether it's home-friendly
    *   Specific tags (e.g., PPL, Cardio)
    *   The system also automatically tries to hide exercises contraindicated for any diseases you've listed in your profile.

### Viewing Exercise Details
*   Click on any exercise name to see its detail page. This includes:
    *   Description, instructions.
    *   A representative image (if available).
    *   Link to a demonstration video (if available).
    *   Difficulty, equipment, primary/secondary muscles, tags.
    *   A warning message if the exercise might be unsuitable based on your registered health conditions.

### Contributing to Exercises
1.  On an exercise detail page, if you're logged in, you'll see a link like 'Add your own image/video for this exercise'.
2.  Click it to go to the contribution form.
3.  You can upload an image, a video file, or provide a link to an external video (e.g., YouTube). Add any notes if you wish.
4.  Submit your contribution. It will be sent for admin review.

### My Contributions
*   Find the 'My Contributions' link in the navigation (usually near your profile/logout links).
*   This page lists all the exercise media you've submitted and their current status (Pending, Approved, Rejected).

## 3. Nutrition

### Browsing Recipes
*   Click 'Nutrition' in the navigation.
*   You'll see a list of all meal recipes. You can filter them by category (e.g., Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) using the dropdown.
*   You can also view a list of all nutrition categories.

### Viewing Recipe Details
*   Click on a recipe name to see its details:
    *   Submitted by, date, image (if available).
    *   Description, prep/cook times, servings.
    *   Ingredients list.
    *   Step-by-step instructions.
    *   Link to a video (if available), which may include an embedded player for YouTube videos.

### Adding Your Own Recipes
1.  Ensure you are logged in.
2.  On the main recipe list page, click 'Add New Recipe'.
3.  Fill out the form:
    *   Name, description.
    *   Ingredients (list one per line).
    *   Instructions (step-by-step).
    *   Prep time, cook time, servings (optional).
    *   Select a nutrition category.
    *   Upload an image of the meal (optional).
    *   Add a video URL (e.g., to a cooking demonstration on YouTube) (optional).
4.  Click 'Save Recipe'. It will appear in the main list.

### Editing/Deleting Your Recipes
*   You can only edit or delete recipes that you have added.
*   On the detail page of a recipe you own, you'll see 'Edit Recipe' and 'Delete Recipe' buttons.

## 4. Calculators
BMI, BMR, and Daily Calorie Needs calculators are integrated into your profile and dashboard.
*   When you update your weight, height, age, sex, and activity level in your profile and save, these metrics are automatically recalculated.
*   The latest results are shown on your Dashboard and Profile page.

## 5. Mobile Use
FitMe is designed to be responsive, meaning it should adapt to different screen sizes, including mobile phones and tablets. The navigation will change to a 'hamburger' menu on smaller screens.
